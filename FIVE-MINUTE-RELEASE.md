# 1.1.10 (22): continuous five-minute nags

Integrates the five-minute hourly-phase method and nil-trigger card preservation confirmed by the user on iPhone 11 Pro / iOS 17.4.1 in Card Guard Probe. Production app identity, reminder database schema, sounds, UI and action ordering remain unchanged.

- A five-minute reminder uses twelve hourly phases sharing one request identifier. The initial fire starts at the reminder time (or the next five-minute slot if already overdue). Snooze creates a new generation starting fifteen minutes later. Done cancels that generation; the existing lifecycle advances daily/yearly/etc. series.
- A complete accepted group is reused on refresh. Five-minute groups, one-minute repeats and other reminders share the existing 60-entry budget. First alerts are reserved before assigning eleven additional entries per continuous group. When a full group cannot fit, ordinary queued five-minute alerts are used with a visible capacity warning. Other nag intervals retain their existing behavior.
- Schedule replacement archives the original and desired requests, pauses pending requests, protects delivered cards with unchanged content, verifies nil triggers and original dates, and only then installs the complete schedule. It never calls removeAllDeliveredNotifications or recreates a dismissed card.
- Relative one-shot countdowns are converted using their saved absolute due times, not restarted. Unrecognized timing aborts the transaction. Unknown reminder records are retained rather than inferred deleted. Explicit stopped generations/deletions are respected during recovery.
- The app blocks conflicting database commits during the critical transaction, waits around imminent deliveries, checks every phase (not just identifier sets), and stores a separate durable recovery journal. On failure it reports that alerts may be paused, retries a bounded number of times, and resumes from the readable reminder database on reopen. No recovery path replaces or resets the reminder database.

Limitations: this is private iOS behavior verified in the separate device probe, not an atomic OS transaction. Process termination during a paused schedule can interrupt alerts until recovery runs. Production combinations, timezone/DST transitions and reboot persistence still need device testing. A successful build cannot establish Lock Screen behavior. Export a backup before installing; update the existing app rather than uninstalling it.

Device regression: existing cards + future reminder; two five-minute reminders; edit an unrelated reminder; Snooze 15 minutes; Done on a daily reminder; +60/+65 minutes; close/reopen without unnecessary re-arming. Verify one card per five-minute reminder and preservation of unrelated cards. Diagnostics distinguish pending phase counts from visible cards.
